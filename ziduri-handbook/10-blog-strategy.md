# 10 — Content and Blog Strategy
Editorial pillars:
Engineering · Architecture · Java · Python · Android · Backend · AI · Productivity · Tutorials

Prioritize original, useful material over publishing volume.

A major product should have a content cluster:
- product landing page
- beginner/use-case articles
- problem-solving articles
- honest comparisons where useful
- troubleshooting/documentation content

Each article needs unique metadata, canonical URL, author/date information and appropriate Article structured data. Link naturally to relevant products.
