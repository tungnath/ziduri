# 09 — Privacy Policy Framework
Each product has its own privacy page, generated from a reusable template but written to reflect actual behavior.

Cover:
- data processed
- purpose
- processing/storage location
- retention/deletion
- third parties
- analytics
- authentication/payment providers
- applicable user rights
- contact process
- effective date/version

Never claim zero collection if the implementation collects anything.

For browser extensions, explicitly document permissions and why they are necessary.
