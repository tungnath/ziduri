# 16 — Analytics and Measurement
Primary sources:
1. Search Console — impressions, queries, clicks, indexing.
2. Analytics — acquisition, engagement, product CTA conversion.
3. Product/store analytics — installations and retention where available.

Useful events may include product_cta_click, documentation_open, store_click and privacy_open.

Minimize personal data.

SEO KPIs: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, branded/non-branded queries, organic product traffic and organic-to-product conversion.

Never promise first-page rankings; SEO depends on competition, relevance, authority and search-engine behavior.
