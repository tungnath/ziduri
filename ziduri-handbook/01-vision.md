# 01 — Vision and Product Philosophy
## Mission
Build small, useful, dependable software that removes friction from everyday digital work.

## Principles
1. Lightweight and fast.
2. Solve a clear recurring problem.
3. Simple by default.
4. Minimize data collection and dependencies.
5. Premium, quiet visual polish.
6. Accessible to keyboard, screen-reader, touch and varied-contrast users.
7. Durable technology over novelty.
8. Every product has a maintenance and lifecycle plan.

Ziduri is a product studio, not a directory of unrelated projects.
