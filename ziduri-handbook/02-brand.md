# 02 — Brand System
## Personality
Elegant · Modern · Confident · Refined · Approachable

## Visual direction
Refined typography, generous whitespace, soft gradients as accents, subtle depth/layering, restrained shadows, strong hierarchy and deliberate micro-interactions.

## Avoid
Visual noise, excessive neon, heavy glassmorphism, gratuitous animation, clutter and generic AI-generated aesthetics.

## Voice
Clear, concise, helpful and technically credible. Benefits before implementation details. Never manufacture urgency or overstate claims.
