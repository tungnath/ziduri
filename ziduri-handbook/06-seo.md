# 06 — SEO Master Strategy
Every indexable page must have a unique title, meta description, clear H1, canonical URL, semantic headings, crawlable HTML, Open Graph metadata, social card metadata, appropriate JSON-LD, descriptive alt text and internal links.

Site-wide:
- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml
- HTTPS
- mobile-first rendering
- clean status codes
- no accidental noindex
- custom 404

Use only truthful schema.org types. SoftwareApplication and BreadcrumbList are candidates where appropriate. Never fabricate reviews, ratings, prices or claims.

Keyword strategy: one primary search intent per landing page; use natural synonyms and related concepts. No keyword stuffing.

Set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap and request indexing for important URLs.

India: do not use artificial geo-targeting. Relevance, quality and authority matter more than a country-code domain alone.

Build topical authority with genuinely useful supporting content and internal links.
