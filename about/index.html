About

Software that respects your time and your data

Ziduri is a product studio building small, dependable software for the parts of digital work that repeat every day — a note, a shortcut, a utility that should have existed already.

Why Ziduri exists

Most software gets heavier over time — more accounts, more permissions, more screens between you and the thing you actually wanted to do. Ziduri is built against that drift. Every product starts from one clear, recurring problem, and stays scoped to solving it well.

How we build

A short set of principles guides every product, regardless of platform:

  • Lightweight and fast. A tool should feel instant, not heavy.
  • One problem, solved well. We'd rather ship a focused utility than a bloated suite.
  • Minimal data collection. We collect only what a product needs to function, and we document it plainly.
  • Durable over trendy. We choose technology built to last, not what's fashionable this year.
  • Accessible by default. Keyboard, screen-reader and varied-contrast users are part of the first draft, not an afterthought.
  • A lifecycle plan for every product. Nothing ships without a plan for how it's maintained.

What we're building toward

Ziduri's first releases are Float Note, a browser extension, LiteView, a desktop image viewer, and two web apps: Ephemera and Anon Postal. The portfolio is designed to grow deliberately from there — across Android apps, developer tools, AI tools, plugins and SaaS — built for developers, students, office professionals, businesses and general consumers.

Status

Ziduri is early. Four products are shipping so far, with more in progress; the studio, the site and the roadmap will keep evolving in the open as more ships.
