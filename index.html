Philosophy

Why Ziduri

Every Ziduri product is held to the same short list of principles, not a marketing checklist.

Lightweight and fast

Minimal footprint by default. Software should feel instant, not heavy.

Solves one problem well

Each product targets a clear, recurring problem rather than trying to do everything.

Minimal data collection

We collect only what a product genuinely needs to function, and we say so plainly.

Durable over trendy

We favor technology that will still work well in five years over what's fashionable today.

Accessible by default

Keyboard, screen-reader and varied-contrast users are considered from the first line of code.

Built with a lifecycle plan

Every product ships with a maintenance plan — no abandoned tools left to quietly rot.
