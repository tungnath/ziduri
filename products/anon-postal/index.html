Anon Postal: share anonymously, save privately, expire automatically

A lightweight home for quick notes, public stories, and private workspace posts. Publish a public post in seconds with no account, or sign in with Google to keep notes in a personal dashboard with a custom expiry window.

Open Anon Postal How it works

Anonymous posts always expire after 24 hours. See the FAQ below for exactly what's public, private, and permanent.

Problem & solution

A quick note shouldn't require an account

Most places to jot down a thought and share it either demand a sign-up first or keep it forever whether you want that or not. Anon Postal separates the two concerns cleanly: publish anonymously and instantly for anything short-lived, or sign in only when you actually want a private, organized, longer-lived workspace.

Features

What Anon Postal does

Anonymous publishing

Write a post and publish it publicly with no account — it gets a short-lived link and expires automatically after 24 hours.

Private workspace

Sign in with Google to keep posts — public or private — in a personal dashboard with editing and deletion.

Real expiry, not just a label

Signed-in users choose 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or never; the choice becomes a fixed timestamp the app actually enforces.

Author identity stays hidden

Public posts never reveal who wrote them — not even to other signed-in users — only whether a post is yours.

Expired means gone

Expired posts disappear from listings immediately, and are permanently deleted from the database by a recurring cleanup job.

Light and dark themes

The interface follows your system theme, with a manual toggle available.

Use cases

Who Anon Postal is for

A quick public note

Share a short story, thought, or announcement without creating an account, knowing it'll expire on its own.

A personal scratchpad

Sign in to keep private drafts and notes organized in one dashboard, with full control to edit or delete them.

Time-boxed sharing

Publish something you only want visible for a set window — an hour, a week, a month — without remembering to take it down.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Anon Postal?

Not for a public post — write one and publish it with no sign-in. An account (via Google) is only needed for private posts, choosing a custom expiry window, and editing or deleting a post later.

How long do anonymous posts last?

Always 24 hours. Anonymous posts are fixed to a 24-hour expiry regardless of any expiry option shown in the interface — only signed-in users can choose a different window.

Can other people tell who wrote a public post?

No. The identity of a post's author is never exposed by the app to other viewers, even for signed-in users' public posts — the app only ever reveals whether a post belongs to the current viewer, never who anyone else's posts belong to.

What happens when a post expires?

It stops appearing anywhere immediately, and is then permanently deleted from the database by a recurring cleanup job.

Can I delete an anonymous post before it expires?

No. An anonymous post isn't tied to any account, so there is nothing to authenticate a deletion request against. It will expire automatically within 24 hours.
